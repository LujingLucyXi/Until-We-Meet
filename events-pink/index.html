The Afternoon

这个下午有什么

这个下午，在 Vermillion Art Gallery and Bar（1508 11th Ave, Seattle 98122）幽微精致的艺术空间里，你将手持一杯无酒精特调，在轻食、破冰宾果与互动表演的欢笑中，自然地认识新朋友。现场以贴纸标明交友意向，来意一目了然；没有速配轮桌，也没有硬聊环节，全程自由走动、自由聊天，最后以一封心动密令，为这个下午留下一点神秘的韵脚。现场吧台可另购鸡尾酒（需出示 ID）。

How It Works

怎么玩

三步，零门槛。所有的设计，都是为了让开口变得容易，让拒绝变得体面。

1

贴上你的爱心贴纸

入场领名字贴和编号，再选一枚爱心贴纸表明来意：白色爱心，轻松交友；粉色爱心，恋爱接触；红色爱心，诚心寻找人生搭子。先看懂来意，聊天就轻松。

2

玩游戏，攒积分

端着特调饮料自由走动，用宾果卡上的小任务去认识新朋友。每完成一条记 1 分，结尾按分数颁奖。

3

心动密令（可选）

把想说的话、想留的言写给 TA——将 TA 的编号编成摩斯密令（ · — · — ），连同你的联系方式投进心动箱；活动后，我们把你的联系方式和留言一起推送给 TA。这不是一个环节，只是一个选项。

The Afternoon

What the Afternoon Includes

This afternoon, in the dim, exquisite art space of Vermillion Art Gallery and Bar (1508 11th Ave, Seattle 98122), you will hold a zero-proof specialty drink and meet new people naturally amid the laughter of curated bites, icebreaker bingo, and interactive performances. Stickers signal each person’s dating intention, so intentions are clear at a glance. No speed-dating rotations, no forced small talk — move freely, talk freely, all afternoon — and close with a Heart Code, leaving a touch of mystery lingering over the afternoon. Cocktails are available for purchase at the on-site bar (ID required).

Specialty Drink · Curated Bites

Every ticket includes one non-alcoholic specialty drink, and curated bites are served all night — graze while you mingle. Cocktails are available for purchase at the on-site bar (ID required).

Icebreaker Bingo

Everyone gets a bingo card: small prompts that walk you up to new people, points for each one you complete, and a prize for the top score. Your heart sticker states your intention up front, so starting a conversation takes no guessing.

Interactive Performance

Short live performances woven between the conversations, so the energy never dips.

Heart Code

Write what you wanted to say — or the note you'd like to leave — for that one person, encode it in Morse, and drop it into the Heart Code box. After the event, we pass it to them together with your contact. Or don't write one. That's fine too.

Viral egg tarts · delicate snacks · one non-alcoholic specialty drink · Seattle stand-up comedians, and more surprises waiting for you.

How It Works

How It Works

Three steps, zero threshold. Everything is designed to make starting a conversation easy — and declining one, graceful.

1

Wear your heart sticker

At check-in you get a name tag and a number, then pick a heart sticker to state your intention: a white heart — just here to make friends; a pink heart — open to a romantic connection; a red heart — seriously looking for a life partner. Intentions read at a glance, and conversations start easy.

2

Play, and collect points

Drink in hand, move freely and use the prompts on your bingo card to meet new people. Each completed prompt earns 1 point, and prizes go to the top scores at the end of the night.

3

Heart Code (optional)

Write what you'd like to say to that person, encode their number in Morse ( · — · — ), add your contact, and drop it into the Heart Code box. After the event, we deliver your message and your contact to them. It is an option, never a step you have to take.

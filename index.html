我们的宗旨

Glomere 的宗旨

别人卖匹配，Glomere 卖主题。当一群人因为同一个主题走进同一个房间，认识彼此就不再需要硬聊。

01

主题先行

每一场都有明确的主题。主题是全场共同的语言，也是最自然的破冰方式：你先属于这个房间，社交随后发生。

TH
02

轻松加入

不用组局，不用凑人，不用认识任何人。看到喜欢的主题，买票就来；一个人来是常态，现场自然有人和你聊起来。

GO
03

精选房间

酒庄、酒吧、天台、画廊，每一个场地都提前踩过点：光线、音乐、动线与私密感，决定了一晚上的化学反应。房间选对了，人自然就放松了。

Our Mission

What Glomere stands for

Others sell matching. Glomere sells the theme. When a group of people walks into the same room for the same theme, getting to know each other stops being awkward.

01

Themed first

Every gathering has a clear theme. A theme is the room's shared language, and the most natural icebreaker there is: you belong to the room first, and the socializing follows.

TH
02

Easy to join

No organizing, no rounding people up, no need to know anyone. See a theme you like, buy a ticket, show up. Coming alone is the norm, and the room takes it from there.

GO
03

Curated rooms

Wineries, bars, rooftops, galleries — every location is scouted in advance. Light, music, flow and privacy decide the chemistry of a night. Get the room right, and people relax.

RM
How It Works

Joining takes three steps

1

Pick a theme

Browse upcoming gatherings and choose a theme you genuinely care about. Right theme, right people.

2

Grab a ticket

Book online. Your e-ticket arrives by email within 5 days before the event, with the venue address attached.

3

Just show up

Leave the rest to the theme and the room: games, performances and the people around you make conversation happen.
